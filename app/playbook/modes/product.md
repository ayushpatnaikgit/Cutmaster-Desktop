# Mode: product demo (16:9)

Screen recordings and product walkthroughs. People want to see exactly what to
click and why it matters.

- **Zoom to what matters.** Follow the cursor: a smooth push-in (1.0 → 1.6–2.2)
  on the area being used, eased, held while it's used, then back out. Never
  jump; never make people hunt for the cursor.
- **Clicks are visible:** a soft ring at each click, and the cursor slightly
  enlarged and smoothed if the recording is jittery.
- **Callouts for features:** a short label (2–5 words) pointing at the UI
  element as it's named, arriving with the word, gone when it's no longer
  relevant.
- **Cut the waiting:** speed through loading, typing and scrolling (2–4×) with
  a small "▸▸" marker, or cut it outright.
- **Structure:** a 3-second title with what the demo shows, one chapter card
  per feature, and an end card with the one action to take next (a URL).
- **Voice:** if there's no narration, write short on-screen captions for each
  step instead; if there is, time everything to it.
