# Mode: talk · lecture (16:9)

Keynotes, lectures and seminar talks. The audience wants to follow an argument.

- **Build the argument visually.** Each main point gets a graphic that stays
  up while it's discussed and builds as sub-points are made (a list whose items
  arrive as spoken, a diagram that gains parts, a chart whose series appear in
  order). Think well-designed slides, animated in time with the words.
- **Signposting:** a small persistent "part 2 of 4 · <topic>" marker in a
  corner when the talk has sections, and a section card at each transition.
- **Equations, quotes and definitions** go on screen verbatim, typeset
  properly (use KaTeX-style layout for maths in the HTML graphic).
- **If there are slides in the footage,** don't redraw them badly — crop to
  them, or rebuild only the key ones cleanly.
- **End with a summary card:** the three to five takeaways, as they were said.
