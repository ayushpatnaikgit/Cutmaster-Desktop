# Mode: podcast (16:9)

Long conversations, often two or more people. The edit serves listening: clean
audio, clear who's talking, easy to navigate.

- **Audio first.** Sync every mic, level them against each other, remove long
  silences and crosstalk, keep the natural rhythm of conversation (don't cut
  every pause — only dead air over ~1.5 s).
- **Who's talking:** a lower-third name card the first time each person speaks,
  and again after a long stretch. If there are separate camera angles, cut to
  whoever is talking.
- **Graphics are sparse:** only when a number, a name, a place or a reference
  comes up that people would want to see. Most of the video is the people.
- **Chapters matter more than anything else:** one per topic change, written
  as a question or a claim ("Why rates stayed high"), in `out/youtube.md`, and
  a small chapter title on screen at each change.
- **Length:** keep it; don't shorten the conversation unless asked. Do suggest
  (in the plan) two or three 30–60 s moments that would make good clips.
